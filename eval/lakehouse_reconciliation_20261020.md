## Drive drain hold and quota evidence

The new GCS-to-Drive drain has **not run**: before the hold its service was inactive, its journal had no entries and its first timer event was scheduled for 20:00 UTC. The owner stopped only `catbird-gcs-drive-drain.timer`; afterward both timer and drain service were inactive, no event remained scheduled and the buffer service remained active. The GCS monitor now explicitly forbids automatically re-enabling the drain.

The drain has a local lock and reactive quota backoff, but no shared rolling-byte admission ledger and no explicit global request pacing. `--max-segments 8` is not an account-wide quota guarantee. The older `RollingQuotaLedger` and `rclone --tpslimit 2` code is a separate implementation; it does not automatically govern this API path or other writers.

The legacy GCE completion ledger contains 3,671 entries dated within the preceding 24 hours, totaling **996,463,022,304 payload bytes**. This is **not** an authoritative amount newly uploaded: cached reuse can receive completion timestamps, receipt/Parquet bytes and uncertain uploads are absent, and other writers are not included. Its maximum three segment completions in one second likewise does not establish the actual file-creation rate. It cannot establish remaining allowance under the 700 GiB cap.

Evidence: `~/catbird-lake-local/graph-build/out/drive_quota_observation_20261020T1839Z.json` and `drive_drain_hold_20261020T1840Z.txt`. Filenames are observation labels; the records carry actual times. GCS continues within $15 storage/operations, $60 compute, cumulative 1 TiB admissions, $250.82 reserved credits and the absolute **2026-10-21 05:29:29 UTC** stop deadline.

Re-enable only after (1) all archive writers and recent/uncertain upload bytes are reconciled, (2) a single shared admission authority reserves payload, receipt and uncertain bytes before sending, (3) requests are globally paced at least 0.5 seconds apart, and (4) release is explicitly authorized. Where other writers cannot participate, retain one writer and hold the others; unknown history must be resolved or conservatively aged out.

2. Specify shared quota accounting/pacing and test it offline with numeric fixtures, including concurrent reservations, unknown outcomes and rolling-window boundaries. No live upload is necessary to validate those state transitions.
