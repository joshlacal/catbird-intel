# Moderation and topic labeler research — 20 October 2026

This research identifies four additional Bluesky labelers worth a bounded Catbird data-quality pilot, alongside Bluesky's own moderation service as a reference. Identity documents, actual `app.bsky.labeler.service/self` records and one-label query probes were read at **2026-10-20 13:08–13:09 UTC**. No posts, images, audio, video, private reports or bulk label histories were downloaded; no paid calls or mutations occurred. A successful one-row query establishes access only, not freshness, completeness, accuracy, training permission or a downloadable full history.

The recommendation is a **source-separated weak-supervision pilot**, not an indiscriminate merged training bank. Account labels must not become labels for every post by that account. Political topic labels below concern post content; person-level ideology inference and sensitive demographic labels are excluded from the proposed Catbird training scope.

## Freshness, retractions and acquisition contract

The [official label specification](https://atproto.com/specs/label) defines separately signed labels and query/subscription transport. For every acquired label preserve `src`, `uri`, optional `cid`, `val`, `cts`, `neg`, optional `exp`, signature and fetch/source cursor provenance. Validate each label against the issuer's label signing key; re-resolve on rotation. Fold negations and expiry; scope CID labels to the matching content version. A negative label operation retracts an earlier decision—it does not prove the opposite class.

Proposed acquisition sequence, after terms/access review:

1. Save each source declaration CID and normalize only an explicit allowlist of labels; reject duplicate definitions until resolved. Record source-specific target semantics and annotation method as unknown unless documented.
2. Begin a bounded query pilot, persist each response hash, cursor and acquisition time, and measure post/account proportions, class counts, duplicate/negation/expiry rates and date coverage. Query order and a one-row result do not establish a complete history.
3. Establish a live subscribeLabels cursor and reconcile its overlap with query pages. Test service-specific cursor reset and missing-history behavior; never promise cursor=0 history merely because the protocol permits it.
4. Join **existing permitted local post text** only after deletion, visibility and label-expiry filters. Do not use a fresh unrestricted scrape to fill gaps. Missing, inaccessible or deleted subjects remain missing, with exclusion counts.
5. Maintain deletion/negation overlays for the derived bank; a later label retraction invalidates its training row. Save a ledger watermark and policy CID with each frozen bank. Split training/development/test by author and time, and keep source-held-out diagnostics.

No labeler declares recall of its coverage. **Absence of a label is not a negative example.** Moderation is selected by reports, automated triggers and operator policy; Ozone is software, not proof of human annotation. Build negative examples through independent annotation of a natural sample. Human reviewers should be blinded to source labels before deciding the target rubric. Report source-specific precision and disagreement instead of merging numerical confidence that the services never supplied.

- **Hailey's labeler:** the verified `hailey.at` account has no labeler endpoint or self declaration in this check; do not guess an alternate identity. The [author's AI-labeler repository](https://github.com/haileyok/ai-labeler) describes a post-scoped provenance detector with query/subscription endpoints, but a repository is not proof of a deployed dataset. Its documented threshold also biases selection to image posts with observed engagement. Treat as an implementation lead, not a source ready for acquisition.
- **Historical `aimod.social`:** not promoted on directory/reddit reputation. A current serving endpoint and valid policy must be verified first; no operational claim is made here.

## Implications for the $200 labeling plan

Spend a small first tranche on **independent disagreement adjudication**, not paying an LLM to restate all existing labeler decisions. Use permitted post-level labels to propose candidates, with source and modality kept separate, and reserve an independently sampled natural-prevalence set. The remaining budget should expand only the targets whose measured pilot precision, annotation agreement, licensing and availability pass. No cost-per-post, throughput, or affordable row-count claim is made here: those require the actual chosen API price, observed token distributions, retry rate and a hard budget guard.

This report recommends four additional useful operators; none is yet qualified as commercial training data. A broad public metadata backfill, model training permission, and production moderation subscription are three separate decisions. No paid calls, user messaging, bulk download, live service change or model inference was performed by this research task.
